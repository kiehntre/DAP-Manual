# Chapter Index and Editorial Roadmap

The skeleton pass is a foundation, not a finished book.

This chapter is the skeleton for tracking the book's editorial state: what exists, what needs expansion, what needs fact-checking and where images, citations and cross-links belong.

## Chapter Status

Later passes should mark each chapter as:

- skeleton;
- expanded draft;
- fact-checked;
- illustrated;
- edited;
- ready for publication.

## Editorial Passes

1. Expand thin chapters.
2. Add citations and official links.
3. Add screenshots and diagrams.
4. Cross-link related chapters.
5. Human edit for rhythm and warmth.
6. Technical review.
7. Final proof.

## High-Risk Chapters

These need extra review:

- current or recent platforms;
- legal landscape;
- firmware and keys;
- store closures;
- emulator project status;
- performance recommendations.

> **DAP Warning**
>
> A skeleton chapter is not a publishable chapter. It is a promise to future editing.
