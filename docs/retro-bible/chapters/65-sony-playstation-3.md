# Sony PlayStation 3

The PlayStation 3 is a serious emulation workload and a serious preservation target.

This chapter is the skeleton for RPCS3, firmware, disc and digital organisation, patches, updates and realistic performance expectations.

## History

The PS3 arrived with ambitious hardware, Blu-ray, the PlayStation Network era and a famously unusual Cell processor.

## Hardware Overview

- Blu-ray media and digital titles.
- System firmware.
- HDD-installed data.
- Updates, DLC and PSN content.
- Sixaxis and DualShock 3 controllers.

## Why It Mattered

The PS3 sits at the turning point where physical media, digital stores, patches and online services became tightly interwoven.

## Preservation Status

RPCS3 has made remarkable progress, but compatibility and performance are title-specific.

## Recommended Emulator

RPCS3 is the standard recommendation.

## BIOS Requirements

RPCS3 uses official system firmware obtained from Sony. Later passes should cite current official instructions.

## Recommended File Formats

- Disc dumps in recognised folder or ISO workflows depending on emulator support.
- PKG-style content for digital titles where legally preserved.
- Keep RAP/licence handling legal and private.

## Folder Layout

```text
/mnt/games/roms/ps3/
/mnt/games/updates/ps3/
/mnt/games/dlc/ps3/
/mnt/games/saves/ps3/
```

## Enhancements

RPCS3 patches can improve resolution or frame rate for some games. Compatibility notes are essential.

## Common Problems

- Missing firmware.
- Game not installed properly.
- Update mismatch.
- RAP or licence confusion.
- CPU performance too weak.

> **DAP Tip**
>
> Treat each PS3 game like a small PC port: record emulator version, firmware, patches, settings and performance notes.

## DAP Gold Standard Setup

- RPCS3 installed and versioned.
- Firmware source documented.
- Per-game compatibility notes.
- Saves and emulator config backed up.

## What Comes Next

Next comes PSP, where portable disc images and standalone handheld design make a very different preservation shape.
