# Nintendo Switch

The Switch is both historically important and legally sensitive because it is still close to the present.

This chapter is the skeleton for documenting Switch preservation with care: no keys, no firmware distribution, no shortcuts around ownership, and no pretending current-platform emulation is the same as preserving a 30-year-old cartridge.

## History

Nintendo fused handheld and home console play into one machine. The Switch changed where and how many people played console games.

## Hardware Overview

- Hybrid handheld and docked console.
- Cartridge and digital software.
- Updates and DLC are normal.
- Joy-Con, Pro Controller and touch input.

## Why It Mattered

The Switch made handheld-first console gaming mainstream again and became home to both new releases and ports from older Nintendo platforms.

## Preservation Status

Technically active and legally delicate. This chapter must be fact-checked carefully in later passes.

## BIOS Requirements

Switch emulation workflows involve firmware and keys from owned hardware. The book must not provide or link to copyrighted firmware, keys or infringing material.
