# AI Restoration and Upscaling

AI tools can help restoration work, but they can also sand the soul off old art.

This chapter is the skeleton for using AI upscaling, frame interpolation, texture restoration and audio cleanup with restraint.

## Historical Context

Upscaling used to mean filters and hand-made packs. Machine-learning tools changed the scale of what could be processed, but not the need for taste.

## Practical Advice

- Keep originals.
- Label AI-generated assets clearly.
- Prefer human-reviewed packs.
- Test in motion, not only screenshots.
- Avoid replacing historical material with speculative output.

## Preservation Notes

AI-enhanced material is derivative. It may be useful for play, but it is not a clean preservation source.

> **DAP Warning**
>
> Never overwrite original assets or clean dumps with AI-enhanced versions. Enhancements belong beside the source, not on top of it.

## Common Mistakes

- Upscaling everything because the tool exists.
- Ignoring art direction.
- Mixing AI and hand-drawn textures inconsistently.
- Forgetting licences for generated or trained material.
- Judging only still images.

## Troubleshooting

### Textures look strange in motion

Check mipmaps, animation frames, UI elements and whether the pack mixed different generation settings.

### Audio restoration sounds artificial

Compare with original output and reduce processing. Clean is not always better.
