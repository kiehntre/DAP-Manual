# Digital Stores, Lost Services and Delisting

Digital games can vanish from sale long before people stop caring about them.

This draft chapter will cover store closures, delisting, patches and service shutdowns as preservation problems with real user impact.

## Historical Context

- Explain the move from physical media to account-bound purchases.
- Cover store closures and partial shutdowns.
- Note patches, DLC and server-dependent features.

## Concepts

| Concept | Meaning | Draft note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delisting | Removal from sale. | Owners may or may not retain access. |
| Store closure | Marketplace shutdown. | Download access varies by platform. |
| DLC | Additional content. | Can be harder to preserve than the base game. |
| Patch | Update data. | May be required for best or fixed versions. |
| Online service | Server-backed feature. | Often disappears first. |

## Practical Setup

- Record what was purchased and where.
- Keep legal ownership notes separate from technical backups.
- Back up patches and DLC where law and platform rules allow.
- Document server-dependent features.
- Preserve manuals, update notes and store metadata where useful.

## Real-World DAP Setup

- Document which digital platforms are relevant.
- Record account-sensitive material outside the public docs.
- Note what can be described without exposing private details.

> **DAP History**
>
> Storefronts are part of game history too. When they close, screenshots, descriptions, patches and download routes can disappear with them.

## Common Mistakes

- Assuming purchase history is a backup.
- Forgetting DLC.
- Ignoring patches after dumping base media.
- Mixing account credentials into notes.
- Treating server shutdowns as only multiplayer problems.

## Troubleshooting

### Purchased item no longer appears

- Check platform download history.
- Check regional account.
- Check delisting notes.
- Check whether only the store page vanished.

### Preserved game lacks DLC

- Check entitlement.
- Check title update requirements.
- Check emulator support.
- Check region match.

## Key Points

- Digital ownership records are fragile.
- Patches, DLC and metadata need attention.
- Avoid putting private account data in the book.
