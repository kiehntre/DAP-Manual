# Nintendo 3DS

The Nintendo 3DS is a handheld built around features that a normal monitor does not naturally have.

This chapter is the skeleton for 3DS preservation, emulator choice, system files, two-screen layouts and realistic compatibility notes.

## History

The 3DS carried Nintendo handhelds into stereoscopic 3D, digital distribution and a long generation of revisions.

## Hardware Overview

- Dual screens.
- Glasses-free 3D top screen.
- Touch bottom screen.
- Cartridge and digital titles.
- Updates, DLC and eShop content.

## Why It Mattered

It closed a long chapter of dedicated Nintendo handhelds before the Switch merged handheld and console identities.
