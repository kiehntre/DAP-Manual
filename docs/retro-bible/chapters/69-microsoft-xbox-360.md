# Microsoft Xbox 360

The Xbox 360 belongs to the era where consoles became online platforms as much as boxes under the television.

This chapter is the skeleton for Xbox 360 preservation, Xenia, updates, DLC and the caution needed around a complex modern-ish ecosystem.

## History

The Xbox 360 defined a major part of HD console gaming, achievements, Xbox Live Arcade and the downloadable-content era.

## Hardware Overview

- DVD-based console.
- Internal or external storage.
- Dashboard and account-linked services.
- Updates, DLC and digital titles.

## Why It Mattered

It made online identity, patches, digital purchases and downloadable games feel central to console life.

## Preservation Status

Developing. Emulation compatibility is title-specific and changes over time.

## Recommended Emulator

Xenia is the main emulator family to document, with Linux status and compatibility checked during editing.

## BIOS Requirements

Traditional BIOS handling is not the main user-facing model, but system data, title updates and content packaging need careful explanation.

## Recommended File Formats

- ISO or extracted formats depending on emulator support.
- XBLA and DLC content needs grouped metadata.

Higher resolution and patches may be possible, but compatibility comes first.

- Missing update.
