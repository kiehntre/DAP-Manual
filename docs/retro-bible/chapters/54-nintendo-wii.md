# Nintendo Wii

The Wii is friendly on the surface and full of little preservation details underneath.

This chapter is the skeleton for Dolphin-based Wii setup, motion controls, RVZ storage, WiiWare and system data.

## History

The Wii pulled motion controls into the living room and reached players who had drifted away from traditional consoles. Its library ranges from simple party games to deep, strange and ambitious work.

## Hardware Overview

- Disc-based console.
- Wii Remote, Nunchuk and sensor bar model.
- Internal NAND storage.
- GameCube compatibility on early hardware.

## Why It Mattered

It changed who console games were visibly for, and it left behind a library that depends heavily on input style.

## Preservation Status

Strong for disc titles. WiiWare, DLC, channels and NAND-related material need more careful notes.

## Recommended Emulator

Dolphin is the standard recommendation.

## BIOS Requirements

No normal BIOS is required for disc emulation. NAND, keys and system files are relevant for some digital and system behaviours.

## Recommended File Formats

- `.rvz` for Dolphin working copies.
- Verified source images kept separately.
- WAD files for channels and WiiWare where legally preserved.

## Folder Layout

```text
/mnt/games/roms/wii/
/mnt/games/verified/wii/
/mnt/games/wiiware/
/mnt/games/saves/wii/
```

## Enhancements

Internal resolution and texture packs can look excellent. Motion-heavy games should be tested for input feel before being marked sofa-ready.

## Common Problems

- Motion controls mapped badly.
- Sensor pointer setup missing.
- WiiWare mixed with disc titles.
- NAND and save paths not backed up.

> **DAP Warning**
>
> A Wii game launching is not the same as a Wii game being playable. Test the actual motion controls the game expects.

## DAP Gold Standard Setup

- RVZ working library.
- Verified sources retained.
- Motion profiles documented.
- NAND and saves backed up.

## What Comes Next

Next comes Wii U, where disc images, digital titles, updates and DLC require a more modern preservation mindset.
