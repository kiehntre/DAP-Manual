# Other Important Systems

No book can pretend the history of games fits only the famous machines.

This chapter is the skeleton for systems that need coverage, research and possibly their own chapters later: PC Engine, Neo Geo, WonderSwan, 3DO, CD-i, Apple II, FM Towns, Sharp X68000, MSX and more.

## Historical Context

Some machines were regional giants. Some were commercial failures with a few remarkable games. Some were development scenes more than mainstream platforms. The skeleton pass should make space for them before polish decides the final depth.

## Practical Advice

- Give each system its own folder even before a full chapter exists.
- Record emulator choice and BIOS needs per system.
- Do not merge obscure systems into "misc".
- Keep regional naming clear.

## Recommended Layout

```text
/mnt/games/roms/pcengine/
/mnt/games/roms/neogeo/
/mnt/games/roms/msx/
/mnt/games/roms/x68000/
/mnt/games/roms/fmtowns/
```

## Preservation Notes

Many of these systems depend on BIOS files, unusual disk formats or regional documentation. Later passes should prioritise primary sources and emulator documentation.

> **DAP Tip**
>
> "Misc" is where future knowledge goes to die. If a system matters enough to keep, it matters enough to name.

## Common Mistakes

- Filing regional systems under arcade or computer without notes.
- Missing BIOS requirements.
- Using a popular emulator because it is convenient rather than accurate.
- Losing manuals for keyboard-heavy systems.

## Troubleshooting

### Game will not boot

Check BIOS, media format, region, emulator compatibility and whether the platform needs boot disks or system cards.
