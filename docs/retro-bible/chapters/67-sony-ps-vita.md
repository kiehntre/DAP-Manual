# Sony PS Vita

The PS Vita is a brilliant handheld caught between physical media, digital stores and a homebrew afterlife.

This chapter is the skeleton for discussing Vita preservation carefully, with strong legal boundaries and realistic emulator notes.

## History

The Vita followed the PSP with a beautiful screen, dual analogue sticks and ambitious portable hardware. It never became the mass-market success Sony wanted, but its library and homebrew scene remain important.

## Hardware Overview

- Proprietary game cards.
- Digital software and updates.
- Touchscreen, rear touchpad and motion controls.
- Account-linked content.

## Why It Mattered

The Vita is one of the clearest examples of why digital store access, updates and handheld-specific controls matter to preservation.

## Preservation Status

Developing. Emulator compatibility and tooling should be fact-checked during later passes.
